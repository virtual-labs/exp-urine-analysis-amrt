### Learning Objectives

1. To learn and interpret the physical characteristics of urine, such as volume, colour, clarity, odor, specific gravity, and pH, to assess hydration status and basic urinary tract health.

2. To perform macroscopic examination of a urine sample using dipsticks or reagent strips to detect semiquantitative constituents such as glucose, proteins, ketone bodies, bilirubin, blood, nitrites, and leukocyte esterase in the given urine sample. 

3. To learn microscopic examination to identify and quantify cellular elements, casts, and crystals in urine sediment to diagnose inflammation, infection, or related renal pathology.

4. To correlate abnormal urine test results with common diseases such as diabetes, kidney disease, and urinary tract infections.
