Refractometer Temperature Correction

VERIFY SG

Refractometers are calibrated at 20°C. Add 0.001 per 3°C above 20°C (or subtract 0.001 per 3°C below 20°C): SG_corr = SG_raw + ((Temp - 20) / 3) × 0.001
